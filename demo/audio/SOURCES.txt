synthetic alarm or silent unlock clip remains in the playback pool.

Changes for this project:
- Public high-quality MP3 previews decoded to mono 24 kHz / signed 16-bit PCM.
- Active voice excerpts cropped to 1.6 seconds; initial silence removed.
- Peak normalized to 0.86 with 12 ms attack / 75 ms release fades.
- Separate source excerpts, some speed/pitch variants and one echo variant.
- Exact excerpt offsets, speed changes and echo flags are in screams.json.
- Ten-item shuffled playback bag; no consecutive repeats across bag boundaries.
- Default playback volume 65%; volume and mute are user-controlled.

vocal-scream.wav is a compatibility copy of scream-female-dry.wav, retained for
the direct preview shared in chat. It is not an extra playback-pool entry.
No runtime internet connection is needed.
